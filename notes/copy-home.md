## The first hundred days — "Itinerary on a slip"

Heading (4 words):
> The first hundred days.

Paragraph beside it (30ch):
> Taking over a business is the moment it is easiest to lose a customer. So the first
> thing we do is nothing that could.

The slip — a real `<ol>`, four stages, **no printed numerals**. Each stage's own title is
its group heading; order is carried by the words and by position.

**Keep what works.** · right column: *Two weeks*
> For the first two weeks we change nothing structural. We confirm cash controls, payer
> access and customer authorizations, keep the manager who knows the business, name a
> deputy, and write down how everything performs today.

**Map the work.** · right column: *One workflow*
> We sit with the team and trace one workflow from start to finish, every handoff, every
> wait, every system. The people doing the work tell us which parts they would gladly
> never do again.

**Prove one thing.** · right column: *Against the baseline*
> One workflow goes live with agents doing the repetitive steps and billers reviewing the
> results, measured against the baseline we wrote down. If quality holds and the work
> costs less end to end, it earns the next step. If not, it stops.

**Make it repeatable.** · right column: *The next business*
> What worked becomes a package: the data, the agents, the rules, the screens, the
> training. The manager owns improvement from here, and the next business starts from the
> package instead of from zero.

Final row, inverted (paper on ink, full width of the slip):
> We judge the first hundred days by customers kept, collections held, and a team that
> trusts the tools. Not by how many agents we shipped.

## Common questions — "Answers as reading text"

Heading (2 words):
> Common questions

Five items, each one paragraph: the question is the run-in in the medium weight, the
answer continues on the same line in the regular weight. No toggles, no boxes.

> **What is Revyve?** Revyve is an AI-native holding company for healthcare services.
> Revyve buys established healthcare services businesses and runs them. We bring capital,
> engineers and operators, and we take responsibility for the result.

> **Does Revyve sell software?** We don't sell these companies software. We own them, so
> we can change who does the work, which steps still need a person, and what the customer
> is promised. It is the platform under every company we own, not a product we sell.

> **Why only healthcare?** We only buy in healthcare. The data rules, the payer behavior
> and the clinical judgment involved are specific enough that a general platform does not
> fit, and two of our four founders practiced medicine. *(link on "two of our four
> founders practiced medicine" → /team/)*

> **Where is Revyve based?** Revyve is headquartered in San Francisco. Our first company
> is a 29-year-old medical billing firm in St. Louis serving independent physician
> practices.

> **Is Revyve raising money?** We are not raising a priced round. If you back
> owner-operators in healthcare, write to us. *(link on "write to us" → /contact/)*
